## What is virgin coconut oil?

Virgin coconut oil is made from the meat of fresh coconuts without chemical refining, bleaching or deodorising. It is not the same as heavily processed “refined” coconut oil.

Ours is cold pressed, so the oil is extracted without high heat. That helps it keep the natural aroma and flavour of the coconut.

## One oil, many everyday uses.

### Cooking and baking

Its mild coconut flavour suits curries, frying, sweets and baking, and it handles everyday cooking heat well.

### Skin care

A natural moisturiser for dry skin. A little goes a long way on hands, elbows and feet.

### Hair care

A traditional Kerala favourite. Massage it into the scalp and lengths before washing for soft, shiny hair.

### Natural medium-chain fats

Coconut oil is rich in medium-chain fatty acids, including lauric acid, which the body handles differently from the long-chain fats in many other oils.

### Naturally unrefined

With no bleaching or deodorising, it keeps natural plant compounds such as vitamin E, along with its fresh scent.

Coconut oil is a saturated fat, so enjoy it in moderation as part of a balanced diet. This information is general and is not medical advice.

## Questions, answered

Why does it turn solid?

Below about 24°C, coconut oil sets naturally. Warm it and it turns back to clear liquid. Both are completely normal.

Is it good for cooking?

Yes. Its mild flavour suits curries, frying and baking.

What does cold pressed mean?

It means the oil is extracted without high heat.

How should I store it?

Keep it in a cool, dry place with the cap closed tight.
